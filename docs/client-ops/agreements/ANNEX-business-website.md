## Deliverables

- Fully functional responsive website (**5–10 pages**)  
- Contact form (spam-protected); call and book-now style buttons  
- Calendar view, booking flow, or **one** third-party integration as scoped  
- Custom design and content structure  
- **Brand kit:** 2 flyers, business card, letterhead, email signature (print-ready files; printing not included)  
- **Google services:** Business Profile create/claim + full setup; Analytics 4 with call/form click tracking; Search Console + sitemap; Client owns accounts; verification guidance; first check-in  
- Launch checklist (DNS, SSL, redirects, forms, analytics) and **30-day post-launch warranty** (Master §8)

**Build complete** = site + brand kit ready for final review / pre-launch. **Launch** after second payment (or pay-in-full).
