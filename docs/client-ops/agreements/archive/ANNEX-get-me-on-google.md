- Google Business Profile create or claim  
- Profile completion: categories, description, hours, services, NAP, photos  
- Verification guidance through Google’s process  
- Google Analytics 4 property setup

- Owner/manager access handoff to Client  
- First monitoring check-in (plain-language review of early Analytics / profile insights)  

## Not included

- Website design or rebuild

- Ongoing monthly SEO retainer (unless quoted separately)  
- Guaranteed ranking or review generation  
- Physical signage or print materials  

## Client responsibilities

- Provide accurate NAP, hours, and ownership proof Google may require  
- Complete postcard / phone / email verification steps Google sends to the business  
- Grant Google account access (or create accounts) so Webstar can configure tools
