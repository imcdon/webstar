## 2. Services and scope

Webstar will perform the **Business Website** services below for this project.

### Included deliverables

- Fully functional responsive website (**5–10 pages** as listed above)  
- Contact form (spam-protected, emailing Client); call and book-now style buttons  
- Calendar view, booking flow, or **one** third-party integration as scoped above  
- Custom design and content structure  
- **Brand kit:** 2 flyers, business card, letterhead, email signature (print-ready files; printing not included)  
- **Google services:** Google Business Profile create or claim and full setup; Google Analytics 4 with call/form click tracking; Google Search Console and sitemap submission; accounts owned by Client’s Google account; verification guidance; first check-in once data arrives  
- Deployment to the hosting path checked above; launch checklist (DNS, SSL, redirects, forms, analytics)

### Not included (unless written change order)

- Unlimited pages or more than one third-party integration  
- Custom application development beyond the agreed booking path  
- Content writing beyond structuring Client-provided copy  
- Physical printing, signage, or ad placement  
- Ongoing hosting, maintenance, SEO campaigns, or paid ads (see Care Plans)  
- Guaranteed Google verification timing, rankings, or reviews  

Anything not listed here or in a written change order is out of scope.
