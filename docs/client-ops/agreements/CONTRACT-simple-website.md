## 2. Services and scope

Webstar will perform the **Simple Website** services below for this project.

### Included deliverables

- Fully functional responsive website (**3 pages**)  
- Contact form (spam-protected, emailing Client)  
- Call and book-now style buttons (links to Client’s phone / existing booking tool)  
- Custom design and content structure based on Client intake  
- **Google services:** Google Business Profile create or claim and full setup (categories, hours, services, description, photos, NAP); Google Analytics 4 property with call/form click tracking; Google Search Console property and sitemap submission; accounts owned by Client’s Google account; verification guidance; one plain-language first check-in once data arrives  
- Deployment to the hosting path checked above; launch checklist (DNS, SSL, forms, analytics)

### Not included (unless written change order)

- Booking/calendar third-party integrations beyond simple links or buttons  
- Print / brand kit (flyers, card, letterhead, signature) — included in Business Website  
- Content writing beyond structuring Client-provided copy  
- Ongoing hosting, maintenance, SEO campaigns, or paid ads (see Care Plans)  
- Guaranteed Google verification timing, rankings, or reviews  

Anything not listed here or in a written change order is out of scope.
