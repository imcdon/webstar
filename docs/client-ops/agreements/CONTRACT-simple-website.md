## 2. Services and scope

Webstar will perform the **Simple Website Package** services below for this project.

### Included deliverables

- Fully functional responsive website (**3 pages**)  
- Contact form  
- Call and book-now style buttons (as fits the design)  
- Custom design and content structure based on Client intake  

Handoff: site files and/or deployment to Client hosting as agreed in writing. Client owns domain/hosting accounts in Client’s name.

### Not included (unless written change order)

- Booking/calendar third-party integrations beyond simple links or buttons  
- Marketing print package (flyers, card, letterhead, signature)  
- Ongoing retainers, SEO campaigns, or paid ads  

Anything not listed here or in a written change order is out of scope.
