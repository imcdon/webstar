## 2. What each plan includes

**Essential Care — $49/mo**
- Hosting on Webstar’s managed server (or management of Client’s own cPanel host)  
- Daily backups with 30-day retention; restore on request  
- Uptime monitoring and SSL certificate renewal  
- PHP, security, and dependency updates  
- **30 minutes** of content edits per month (text, images, hours, prices, simple sections)  
- Email support with a **1-business-day** response target  

**Standard Care — $99/mo** (everything in Essential, plus)
- **60 minutes** of content edits per month  
- Monthly plain-English report from Google Analytics and Search Console  
- Monthly Google Business Profile check and photo/post refresh  
- Priority support with a **same-business-day** response target  

**Premium Care — $179/mo** (everything in Standard, plus)
- **120 minutes** of content edits or small features per month  
- Quarterly strategy call and conversion review  
- Seasonal promo updates and new landing sections  
- Phone and text support during business hours  

**Edit minutes:** tracked in 15-minute increments; unused minutes do not roll over. Work beyond the monthly allowance is quoted in advance as a change order at Webstar’s then-current hourly rate or moved to a higher plan.

**Not included:** domain registration fees; third-party subscriptions (booking software, email marketing, stock photos); redesigns or new page templates beyond the plan’s minutes; paid ads or SEO campaigns; content writing beyond edits; recovery from changes made by Client or third parties outside Webstar’s access (quoted separately); guaranteed rankings or reviews.

---

## 3. Fees and billing

The monthly fee is charged automatically by **Stripe** on the billing day. If payment fails and is not resolved within 15 days, a 1.5% monthly late fee may apply and services may pause (the site stays online for a further 15 days before suspension). Price changes require **60 days’ written notice**.

---

## 4. Term and cancellation

Month-to-month, starting `{START_DATE}`. Either party may cancel with **30 days’ written notice** (email is fine). On cancellation:

- Webstar provides a **complete export** of the site (files, database if any, instructions) within 10 business days, at no charge.  
- If the site is on Webstar’s server, hosting ends at the end of the notice period; Webstar helps repoint DNS to the new host.  
- Any unused edit minutes expire; no partial-month refunds.

Client’s domain, content, Google properties, and (once paid for under the original project agreement) website files remain Client’s at all times.

---

## 5. Access and security

Client grants Webstar the access needed to perform the plan (hosting, DNS, Google properties as manager). Webstar stores credentials in a password manager, never in public repositories, and removes its access within 10 business days of cancellation on request. Backups are stored off-server and encrypted at rest where the provider supports it.

---

## 6. Service levels

Target uptime 99.5% monthly excluding scheduled maintenance announced in advance; monitoring alerts are acted on during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9–5 ET) and for outages as soon as practical. Webstar’s remedy for missed service levels is a pro-rated credit of the monthly fee for the affected days, on request. Client’s own hosting provider’s uptime is outside Webstar’s control.
