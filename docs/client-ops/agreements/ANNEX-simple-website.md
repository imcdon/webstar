## Deliverables

- Fully functional responsive website (**3 pages**)  
- Contact form (spam-protected, emailing Client)  
- Call and book-now style buttons (links to phone / existing booking tool)  
- Custom design and content structure based on Client intake  
- **Google services:** Business Profile create/claim + full setup; Analytics 4 with call/form click tracking; Search Console + sitemap; Client owns accounts; verification guidance; first check-in  
- Launch checklist (DNS, SSL, forms, analytics) and **30-day post-launch warranty** (Master §8)

**Build complete** = site ready for final review / pre-launch. **Launch** after second payment (or pay-in-full).
