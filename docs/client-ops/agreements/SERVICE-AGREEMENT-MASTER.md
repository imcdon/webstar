## 2. Scope of work

1. This Agreement, and

## 3. Client responsibilities

You agree to provide, in a timely manner:

- Logo files, brand colors, and existing copy or photos you want used  
- Accurate business name, contact info, hours, service areas, and legal disclaimers you need on the site or materials  
- Prompt feedback on drafts (target: within **3 business days** of each review request)  
- Access to domain, hosting, or third-party accounts when integration is in scope (or credentials via a secure method you choose)

Delays in assets or approvals may push `{DELIVERY_DATE}`. Webstar is not responsible for delays caused by missing Client materials or late approvals.

## 4. Timeline

Estimated delivery: `{DELIVERY_DATE}`, starting after (a) this Agreement is signed and (b) the first payment (or payment in full) is received, and (c) intake assets needed to begin are provided.

Timelines are estimates, not guarantees. Complex integrations, third-party outages, or scope changes may extend delivery.

## 5. Fees and payment

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total project fee | `{PRICE}` |
| First payment | `{DEPOSIT}` |
| Balance | `{BALANCE}` |
| Payment plan | `{PAYMENT_PLAN}` |

**Payment options (choose one in the Package Contract):**

1. **Pay in full** after signing — work begins after signature and full payment clear.  
2. **Fifty / fifty (50/50):**  
   - **First payment (50%)** due upon signing this Agreement. Production starts after signature and first payment clear.  
   - **Second payment (50%)** due when Webstar notifies Client that the **build is complete** (deliverables ready for final review / pre-launch).  

**After the build is complete and the balance is paid:**

- **Website packages:** Webstar **deploys** the site (or delivers handoff files) as scoped.  
- **Marketing Package:** Webstar delivers final design files (no website deploy).  

**Card on file:** Client authorizes Webstar to keep a payment method on file (via Stripe or another processor Webstar specifies) **until the full invoice amount for this project is paid**. Webstar may charge the remaining balance when due under this Agreement and will notify Client of charges. The card may be removed after the project is paid in full (or earlier only if Webstar agrees in writing).

Invoices are issued via **Stripe** (or another method Webstar specifies in writing). Work may pause if payment is overdue.

Fees do not include third-party costs (domain, hosting, stock photos, SaaS subscriptions, app store fees, etc.) unless the Annex/Contract explicitly says they are included. Those remain Client’s responsibility.

## 6. Revisions

Unless the Annex/Contract says otherwise:

- Package price includes **up to two (2) rounds** of reasonable revisions on deliverables in that package.  
- Additional rounds or major direction changes may be billed at a rate agreed in writing, or as a new quote.

---

## 7. Ownership and accounts

- **You own** your domain registration and hosting accounts when they are in your name.  
- Upon full payment, you own the **final deliverable files** Webstar produces for this project (site files, design exports as specified), subject to third-party licenses (fonts, stock, plugins).  
- Webstar may showcase the work in portfolios and marketing unless you request otherwise in writing before launch.  
- Pre-existing Webstar tools, frameworks, and non-Client-specific code remain Webstar’s; you receive a license to use them as delivered in your project.

---

## 8. Build complete, deploy, and handoff

- **Build complete** means Webstar has finished the agreed build and notified Client that deliverables are ready for final review (or pre-launch), subject to outstanding Client assets.  
- **Deploy / handoff** for website work occurs **after** the second payment clears (or after pay-in-full), per the Package Contract.  
- After handoff, ongoing hosting, renewals, content updates, and security monitoring are Client’s responsibility unless a separate support agreement exists.

---

## 9. Security and privacy

Webstar will take reasonable care with Client credentials and business information, including:

- Not placing plaintext passwords or secrets in public repositories  
- Using staging / preview access controls when Webstar hosts a preview  
- Using secure channels for sharing credentials when possible  
- Using project information only to deliver this engagement; Webstar does not sell Client data  

Client remains responsible for the legality of content Client provides and for Customer data Client collects. After handoff, Client is responsible for their hosting, backups, and ongoing security unless a separate support agreement exists.

Webstar is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages (including lost profits or data), or for issues arising from Client’s hosting, third-party tools, or content Client provides.

Webstar does not guarantee search rankings, lead volume, or business results.

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate in writing if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within **10 days** of notice.  

If Client terminates for convenience after work has begun, Client pays for work performed and non-refundable third-party costs; unused deposit portions may be refunded only as agreed in writing. Deliverables completed and paid for remain subject to Section 7.
