## 2. Scope of work

**Payment options (choose one in the Package Contract):**

1. **Pay in full** after signing — work begins after signature and full payment clear.  
2. **Fifty / fifty (50/50):**  
   - **First payment (50%)** due upon signing. Production starts after signature and first payment clear.  
   - **Second payment (50%)** due when Webstar notifies Client that the **build is complete** (deliverables ready for final review / pre-launch).  
3. **Milestones** (Business Growth only) — the written milestone schedule in the Package Contract controls.

**After the build is complete and the balance is paid:** Webstar **deploys** the site to `{HOSTING_PATH}` (or delivers handoff files) as scoped and completes Google services.

**Card on file:** Client authorizes Webstar to keep a payment method on file (via Stripe or another processor Webstar specifies) **until the full invoice amount for this project is paid**. Webstar may charge the remaining balance when due under this Agreement and will notify Client of charges.

**Late payment:** Amounts unpaid **15 days** after the due date may accrue a late fee of **1.5% per month** (or the maximum allowed by law, if lower). Work may pause while payment is overdue and the timeline extends accordingly.

Invoices are issued via **Stripe** (or another method Webstar specifies in writing). Fees do not include third-party costs (domain, Client hosting, stock photos, SaaS subscriptions, printing, etc.) unless the Annex/Contract explicitly says they are included.

## 6. Revisions and change orders

Unless the Annex/Contract says otherwise:

- Package price includes **up to two (2) rounds** of reasonable revisions on deliverables in that package.  
- **Change orders:** work outside the written scope (new pages, features, integrations, or a change in direction) requires a written change order — email is sufficient — stating the price and any timeline change, approved by Client before Webstar starts it. Change-order work is invoiced on approval unless the change order states otherwise.

## 8. Build complete, launch, warranty, and handoff

- **Build complete** means Webstar has finished the agreed build and notified Client that deliverables are ready for final review (or pre-launch), subject to outstanding Client assets.  
- **Launch / handoff** for website work occurs **after** the second payment clears (or after pay-in-full), per the Package Contract.  
- **30-day post-launch warranty:** for **30 days after launch**, Webstar will fix at no charge any in-scope defect — broken links, form errors, layout bugs, features not working as delivered. The warranty excludes changes made by Client or third parties, hosting outages, third-party service changes, content updates, and new requests.  
- After handoff, ongoing hosting, renewals, content updates, backups, and security monitoring are Client’s responsibility unless a **Care Plan Agreement** or **Website as a Service Agreement** is in place.

## 9. Confidentiality, security, and privacy

- Each party will keep the other’s non-public business information confidential and use it only for this engagement, during the project and for **2 years** after. This does not cover information that is public, already known, or required to be disclosed by law.  
- Webstar will take reasonable care with Client credentials: no plaintext passwords or secrets in public repositories; access controls on staging previews; secure channels for sharing credentials; no selling of Client data.  
- Client remains responsible for the legality of content Client provides and for customer data Client’s site collects. After handoff, Client is responsible for hosting, backups, and ongoing security unless a support agreement exists.
