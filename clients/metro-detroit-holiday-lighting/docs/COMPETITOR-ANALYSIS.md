---

## Market reality

- Buyers expect **design → install → mid-season care → takedown → storage**.
- Book **October–early November** for date choice.
- Two models: **leased lights** (Ho Ho, Let It Glow, GlowHaus) vs **turnkey with company-supplied materials + storage** (Northern Lights, Diamond Shine, MDHL).
- Densest competition: **Oakland** (Birmingham, Bloomfield, Troy, Rochester). HOA/city is a separate lane (Trillium, Mathews).

## Positioning (MDHL)

**Primary message:** Owner-operated holiday lighting for homes, storefronts, and HOA entrances — one expert from design through takedown.

**Do not claim:** “#1,” guaranteed rankings, or review counts without a live source.
